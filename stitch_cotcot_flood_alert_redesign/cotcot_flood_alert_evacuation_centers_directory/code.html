Comprehensive Flood Safety Guide

Step-by-step standard operating protocols formulated by Cotcot BDRRMO for household readiness.

Standard DRRM Checklist
backpack

Prepare Your 72-Hour "Go-Bag"

Pack ready-to-eat rations, 3 liters of potable drinking water per individual, water-purification tablets, and extra prescription medications.

folder_zip

Seal Crucial Documents

Store government IDs, land titles, health records, birth certificates, and insurance policies in heavy-duty waterproof ziplock envelopes.

electrical_services

Elevate Critical Household Utilities

Raise heavy electrical machinery, appliances, gas tanks, and power strips above anticipated inundation flood marks (minimum 1.5 meters from floor).

route

Family Evacuation Route Mapping

Pre-designate an elevated rendezvous waypoint and monitor Cotcot river hydrograph sensors for real-time crest trends.

check_circle Emergency gear checklist: Flashlight, whistle, battery radio, powerbank, hygiene kit. Cotcot BDRRMO S.O.P. #01
power_settings_new

Cut Main Circuit Breaker Immediately

If floodwaters approach your home, shut off main electrical switches and LPG valves prior to water contact to avoid fatal electrocution risks.

no_transfer

Never Walk or Drive in Flood Streams

Just 6 inches of rapid water current can sweep an adult off their feet; 12-18 inches can float and overturn cars and passenger vans.

electric_bolt

Steer Clear of Fallen Utility Poles

Assume all submerged cables and dangling wires carry live voltage. Maintain at least a 10-meter clearance perimeter from fallen hardware.

directions_walk

Orderly Evacuation to Shelters

Proceed promptly to your assigned shelter along elevated Purok arterial routes. Keep children and the elderly secured in front of the line.

priority_high Rule of Thumb: "Turn Around, Don't Drown". Inundation speeds can escalate within 12 minutes. Liloan DRRM Advisory
campaign

Await Official BDRRMO "All-Clear"

Do not return to low-lying riverbanks until the Cotcot Barangay Captain and municipal engineers issue an official clearance decree.

water_drop

Boil Water for Minimum 3 Minutes

Assume municipal pipeline supplies and groundwater shallow wells are contaminated by flood runoff. Boil vigorously before drinking or cooking.

pest_control

Inspect for Structural Shifts & Reptiles

Check foundations, load-bearing walls, and ceilings for fissures. Be vigilant: displaced snakes, rodents, and scorpions often harbor in debris.

photo_camera

Document Damage for Municipal Relief

Take photographs and timestamps of structural damage and lost property prior to cleanup to facilitate DSWD and LGU emergency rehabilitation grants.

health_and_safety Wear thick rubber boots and work gloves during domestic flood cleanups. DSWD Guidelines
